**Problem.** Home workout apps lock their programs behind a pricey subscription, and personal trainers are expensive.

MassBeat was a home workout app concept. It had guided workout programs and paid consultations with personal trainers. It was a design and business plan, never a working app.

We were Tim Syahduu at Politeknik Caltex Riau. Aidel Haikal Alatas was the team lead, for networking, administration, and recruiting respondents. Sari Wahyuni was the business analyst, and I was the UI/UX designer.

## The problem

We started from a survey of 39 respondents, mostly students aged 18 to 23. We also did two interviews, one with a personal trainer in Pekanbaru and one with a person who exercises regularly. The trainer's clients had kept consulting by chat during the pandemic. That supported remote consultation as a feature.

We also compared against Fita, an Indonesian fitness app. Its programs are locked behind a pricey subscription, and its interface is harder to use.

Of 39 respondents, 78% wanted a workout program that fits them. In the same survey, 62% said an app would help them reach their goals, and 50% wanted to consult an expert.

## Decisions

### Scope the first version to the top priority

Ideas went through an affinity diagram and a prioritization matrix. Learning correct form and having a guide came first. Regular programs and healthy meals came later. We delegated rewards, and we eliminated personal calorie and nutrition tracking.

### A free tier plus cheap subscriptions

The free tier has limited programs. Silver costs Rp10,000 a week or Rp40,000 a month, and Gold costs Rp25,000 a week or Rp60,000 a month. Gyms and healthy-food businesses can buy ad packages. This answers the pain point that personal trainers are expensive.

### Mobile first, minimal desktop

I designed the mobile prototype in full, and a reduced desktop version for the web.

## Impact

We ran a usability test with the Single Ease Question, with 5 testers and 7 tasks. The average score was 6.32 out of 7, and the average task time was 12.17 seconds. Every task was completed. Login was easiest (7.0), and subscribing to a package was hardest (5.8). One tester said the subscribe button's color did not fit. Then we ran follow-up interviews from January 14 to 17, 2023.

The plan won three podium places, all in business plan categories, not UI/UX:

- 1st Runner Up, GenBI Riau Business Plan Competition 2023
- 2nd Runner Up, LIKMI #2 2023 (Yogyakarta State University)
- 2nd Runner Up, PIKMI 2023 (Bina Sarana Informatika University)

We were also a finalist at the Sumatera Accounting Competition 2022, and we took part in 1000 Startup Digital.

## How it ended

We joined 1000 Startup Digital to build the real app. We completed its Ideation stage and advanced to Hacksprint. But we could not present or continue because of our college workload, so we sunset the project.
