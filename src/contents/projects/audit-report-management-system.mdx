## The problem

I interviewed a government office staff member who helps manage the assignment reports. I asked about their real flow, what they need, and what kinds of documents they handle.

Staff often lost track of which document was missing from an assignment. They only noticed later, when they needed the assignment's data again. There was no single sheet that listed the documents of every assignment, and no single place to store them. Each person kept their own documents in their own Google Drive.

When the office asked for a document, they handed it over. If a document was lost, that was the end of it. Most of the documents come from monitoring on site, so nobody can make them again.

## Decisions

### A document checklist for every assignment

Each assignment lists the documents it needs, so staff can see what's done and what's missing at a glance. There are six:

- Surat Tugas, the assignment letter
- PKA, the audit work program
- KKA, the audit working papers
- LHA, the audit result report
- NHA, the audit result notice
- Berita Acara, the record of the follow-up agreement

A seventh section, Tindak Lanjut, holds follow-up documents and stays open after an assignment is finished.

### Completion: a strict rule now, a looser one planned

Only the team leader can finish an assignment. Right now an assignment needs all six documents before it can be finished.

That is hard for the office at the moment, because they have already lost many documents. So I plan to loosen the rule for a few months, until they are better prepared and fully moved over. The team leader would tick that the documents match the assignment, and that they are responsible for any that are missing. I haven't built this yet.

### Team members belong to the assignment

Teams rotate every month in practice, and often the same people work on both assignments of a month. So I store the members on the assignment, not on a fixed team.

### Cloudflare R2 for the files

The files live in Cloudflare R2. It has no egress fees, so downloads don't add cost. Its API is S3-compatible, so standard libraries work. The person who offered the system pays for the storage for now. So the office can try it at no cost, and take over the bill once it proves useful.

## What is not done

- The report dashboard is planned and not started. It would filter by category, month, year, or team leader, and export as PNG or PDF.
- Some files are large PDFs. I plan to raise the upload limit to 100 MB. I also plan to upload in chunks, so an upload that stops halfway doesn't restart.
- The user guide is short and has no screenshots or demo yet. I'm improving it.

## How much it's used

Several teams use it. Each team has about 2 assignments a month, and each assignment has 11 to 30 PDFs. Most files are under 10 MB. Staff use it a few times a month, mostly to upload.
