## Design sprint

We assisted a design sprint led by BPPK stakeholders, with 20 to 30 Ministry employees from various units. They were the people who would use the system. We did not just receive a requirements document. Sitting with them showed me the friction in their daily booking work first-hand. It turned into product requirements, business process diagrams, and mockups.

I think the sprint wasn't held really well, though. We ended up designing the UI too quickly, in the middle of defining the problems, because many of the older employees needed something to visualize.

## Scrum

We had dozens of features to build and three developers, so we worked in Scrum with two-week sprints.

- Two-week sprints forced us to break large requirements into small, shippable tasks.
- Each sprint ended with a review where we presented finished work to the project managers. So we could change course early when a feature did not match the need.
- There was no dedicated QA. I tested the flows I built by hand, blackbox style, to check they held up in realistic use.

## Role

I built the Public and Employee flows on the frontend and designed about 80% of the system in Figma. I also helped write the business requirements document, with the ERD, BPMN, and use cases.
